# Privacy

- No internet permission.
- No ads.
- No analytics or tracking SDK.
- No mandatory account.
- No automatic cloud upload.
- No face upload or preview-frame upload.
- No hidden background recording behavior.
- No collection of device identifiers.
- Media is saved locally through MediaStore.
- Microphone permission is requested only for video recording with audio.
- Location permission is not requested in this build.
